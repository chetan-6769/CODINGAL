facts abot monkeys

Monkeys are warm-blooded vertebrate animals belonging to the order Primates. There are more than 260 known species of monkeys in the world. They are mainly found in Africa, Asia, Central America, and South America. Monkeys are divided into two groups: Old World monkeys and New World monkeys. Most monkeys have tails, and some species use them to grip branches. They are omnivores and eat fruits, seeds, leaves, insects, and small animals.
